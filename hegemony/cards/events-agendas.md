# Event and Political Agenda cards (English translation of the unofficial Italian file v2.1 by F. Santoro)

Legend: "Working +1" = +1 on the State's Legitimacy track for that class (circle on the card). "Working token +1" =
one Legitimacy token for that class (pentagon on the card). VP = victory points (PV on the card). "2 of your choice" =
the State picks two of the listed penalties.

- **Task:** Buy Luxury from other players, in any combination, up to the number of public Companies.

## Political Agenda cards (10) — not in this file

The Italian file does not contain the 10 base-game State Political Agenda cards (the ones with target policies). Its
"agende segrete" are the 20 Crisis & Control **Hidden Agenda** cards (listed below in the expansion section), and the

come from the physical cards or another source.

## Crisis & Control Hidden Agenda cards (20) — EXPANSION

The file's "agende segrete". Class by title colour. Format: condition → VP.

**Working Class (red)**
- **Working class push** (Spinta della classe operaia): in the same Voting phase, get 3 Bills you proposed passed → 10 VP
- **Workers, unite!** (Lavoratori, unitevi!): at any time you have at least 4 active Unions → 8 VP
- **The surplus value is ours!** (Il plusvalore è nostro!): receive at least 240₩ in wages in a single Production phase → 8 VP
- **Small labor force** (Piccola forza lavoro): at game end you have at most 7 Population → 5 VP
- **Rally the masses** (Raduna le masse): at any time you have at least 6 Influence → 4 VP

**Middle Class (yellow)**
- **Growing middle class** (Classe media in crescita): at any time you have 8 fully operational Companies (two Workers assigned) and at least 5 of your Workers assigned to public or capitalist Companies → 10 VP
- **Successful startup** (Startup di successo): at any time, but by the end of round 3, you have at least 5 fully operational Companies → 8 VP
- **Entrepreneur** (Imprenditore): at any time you have at least 8 Food, 8 Luxury, 8 Health and 8 Education → 8 VP
- **We work, we thrive** (Noi lavoriamo, noi prosperiamo): at game end you have no unemployed Workers → 5 VP
- **Golden compromise** (Compromesso d'oro): at any time all current Policies 1-5 are in section B (1B 2B 3B 4B 5B) → 4 VP

**Capitalist Class (blue)**
- **Corporate empire** (Impero aziendale): at any time you have 12 Companies, at least 11 of them operational → 10 VP
- **Export tycoon** (Magnate delle esportazioni): in the Action phase, earn at least 240₩ with a single Foreign Market sale action → 8 VP
- **Diversified portfolio** (Portafoglio diversificato): at game end you have at least 2 Companies in each industry (Food, Luxury, Health, Education and Influence) → 8 VP
- **Venture capitalist** (Investitore): at game end you have at least 320₩ in your Capital → 8 VP
- **Toward a neoliberal State** (Verso uno Stato neoliberista): at any time after round 1, all current Policies 1-5 are in section C (1C 2C 3C 4C 5C) → 4 VP (title colour corrected in v2.1)

**State (grey)**
- **Strong Welfare State** (Forte Stato sociale): at any time you have at least 150₩ and current Policies 4 and 5 are in section A (4A, 5A) → 10 VP
- **Leviathan** (Leviatano): at any time you have resolved at least 10 Events → 8 VP
- **State-led economy** (Economia guidata dallo Stato): at any time current Policy 1 is in section A (1A) and you have at least 6 operational public Companies → 8 VP
- **According to plan** (Tutto procede secondo il piano): in the Scoring phase, at least 4 current Policies match your current Political Agenda card → 5 VP
- **Expert budgeter** (Esperto di budget): end the game without ever having taken a loan; if you take one, discard this card → 4 VP

## Uncertain

- **Base-game Political Agenda cards:** not in the source file, so not translated (see that section).
- **Political polarization:** the Working Class reward is printed as a red −1 circle per 2 cubes (in both the image and the text layer). It may be a source error for +1. Kept as printed.
- **Fear of deflation:** the page 41 reprint's reward header says "Spend Education", which contradicts its own task (Spend ₩). The page 31 version is used.
- **Neighbors at war:** the task says "Spend up to 6 Health", but the reward line says "1 VP per Health provided". Read as "per Health spent".
- **Global supply chain crisis:** the card does not say whether "half its Population" is the total of Food and Luxury or the amount of each.
- **Technological illiteracy:** "half its operational Companies" has no rounding rule on the card.
- **VP in a class column** (e.g. "Capitalist Class → 3 VP"): the card does not say who scores. Read as the State's VP.
- **C&C bottom bars:** applying them to the class that took the card is inferred from the wording ("the State pays you").
- **Official names:** the index on page 08 has typos that are fixed here: "Ouotdated" → Outdated, "Companied" → Companies, "Demand fo" → Demand for, "transofrmation" → transformation. "Demand for thirteen month pay" and "Need for sustainability initiative" are given as they appear in the index.
- **Not translated (out of scope):** the 10 C&C IMF cards (pages 38-39) and the corrected action cards on pages 40-41 (only the two event reprints there were checked).
