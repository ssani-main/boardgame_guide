# Middle Class action cards (English translation of the unofficial Italian file v2.1 by F. Santoro)

Total: 45 cards = 40 base + 5 Crisis & Control (C&C). Source: sheets "Classe Media, foglio 1/5 to 5/5".

### Change of Government agenda ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Cambio di agenda governativa
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If there are 3 or fewer Bills on the board, discard one Bill proposed by another player. Then you Propose another Bill. Finally, that player Proposes another Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Covid-19 stimulus package ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Pacchetto di incentivi anti Covid-19
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 2₩ from the State for each assigned Worker you have in Public and Capitalist Companies, plus 4₩ for each Company you own.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 15₩ received

### Employment subsidy ×2
- **Italian:** Sussidio all'occupazione
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Gain 5₩ from the State for each of your Companies with a Working Class Worker. Then you may perform an Extra Shift in one of these Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 3 of your Companies counted by this card

### Export subsidy ×2
- **Italian:** Sussidio all'esportazione
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Sell to the Foreign Market. For each transaction performed this way, gain 5₩ from the State.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Fake news ×1
- **Italian:** Fake news
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Draw 6 Voting cubes from the bag. Return up to 4 of them to the supply and the rest to the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign market insight ×2
- **Italian:** Conoscenza del mercato estero
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Reveal the next Export card. You may replace the current Export card with the one just revealed. Then Sell to the Foreign Market and discard the Export card that was not used. You may perform each transaction **up to 2 times**.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Growing business ×2
- **Italian:** Impresa in crescita
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Build a Storage for free and place it below your board, next to a specific resource. Then you may Build Company. If this Company produces that resource, pay 4₩ less for it.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Health crisis ×1
- **Italian:** Emergenza sanitaria
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell up to 6 Health to the State for 10₩ each.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 3 Health sold

### Healthcare benefits ×1
- **Italian:** Assistenza sanitaria
- **Requirement:** 4B/4C
- **Effect:** Buy Health from the State, up to your Population value, paying half the cost rounded up.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Higher education program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma d'istruzione superiore
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell up to 6 Education to the State for 10₩ each.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 3 Education sold

### Highlight social issues ×1
- **Italian:** Evidenzia i problemi sociali
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Buy 3 Influence from the State for 15₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Immigration ×3
- **Italian:** Immigrazione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If you have at least 4 unemployed Workers, remove up to 2 of them and **decrease your Population** accordingly (not below the minimum). Gain 5₩ for each unskilled Worker and 10₩ for each skilled Worker removed this way.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Immigration reform ×1
- **Italian:** Riforma dell'immigrazione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add 2 of your Voting cubes from the supply to the bag. Then Propose a Bill on immigration (**7. Immigration**).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Import subsidy ×2
- **Italian:** Sussidio all'importazione
- **Requirement:** 6A/6B
- **Effect:** You may buy Food **or** Luxury from the Foreign Market, up to your Population value, without paying any tariff. (One copy adds "(**import area**)" after "Foreign Market"; otherwise the two copies are identical.)
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 4 Food or Luxury bought

### Interest groups ×1
- **Italian:** Gruppi d'interesse
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Reveal Voting cubes from the bag until you draw 3 of other classes. Replace them with as many of your own Voting cubes taken from the supply, and put the latter back in the bag together with the remaining revealed cubes.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Investment opportunities ×2
- **Italian:** Opportunità d'investimento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Build Company paying 8₩ less **OR** search the Company deck for a Company of your choice and build it paying 8₩ more. Afterwards, shuffle the Company deck.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Labor market deregulation ×1
- **Italian:** Deregolamentazione del mercato del lavoro
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Assign any number of unemployed Workers. If **2C** is in effect, you may also reassign any number of already-assigned Workers to other Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Land of opportunity ×2
- **Italian:** La terra delle opportunità
- **Requirement:** 7B/7C
- **Effect:** Build Company using a skilled Middle Class Worker of your choice, **taking it from the supply**.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Momentum for change ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Verso il cambiamento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 1 Influence for each of your Bill markers on the board. Then Propose a Bill. You **cannot** call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### New theme park ×1
- **Italian:** Nuovo parco a tema
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay 6₩ times your current Population value and gain 1 Prosperity.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Personal consumption ×2
- **Italian:** Consumo personale
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Buy Goods & Services from any number of sources, up to your Population value from each, **OR** Buy Goods & Services from a single source, up to twice your Population value.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Protect the status quo ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Proteggi lo status quo
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose a policy. For the rest of this round, **no** Bill may be proposed for it (place one of your Bill markers in the matching in-effect section as a reminder). Then gain 1 Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public opinion polling ×2
- **Italian:** Sondaggio d'opinione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill and reveal 5 Voting cubes from the bag. Then you may call an Immediate Vote on it without spending Influence. If you do, use those 5 revealed Voting cubes instead of drawing others. If you don't, put those cubes back in the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public sector overtime ×1
- **Italian:** Straordinari nel settore pubblico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose an operational Public Company. The State pays the Wages of the Workers assigned there and carries out a production with that Company. Then you may Buy Goods & Services from the State of the type produced by that Company.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Reveal political scandal ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Rivela uno scandalo politico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Take 2 Influence from another player.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Small business grant ×2
- **Italian:** Contributo alle piccole imprese
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Build Company. The State pays its cost.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Specialization ×2
- **Italian:** Specializzazione
- **Requirement:** 7B/7C
- **Effect:** Place a **skilled Working Class Worker** of your choice **from the supply** in the unemployed area. Then Assign up to 3 unemployed Working Class Workers to your Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### State scholarship ×1
- **Italian:** Borsa di studio statale
- **Requirement:** 5B/5C
- **Effect:** Buy Education from the State, up to your Population value, paying half the cost rounded up.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Supplemental income program ×2
- **Italian:** Reddito supplementare
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Gain 2₩ from the State for each of your Workers assigned to Public and Capitalist Companies. Then you may Buy Goods & Services.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1 for every 5 Workers counted by this card

### Unemployment initiative program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma di riduzione della disoccupazione
- **Requirement:** 4A/4B
- **Effect:** Assign up to 3 **unemployed Working Class Workers** to your Companies. Gain 5₩ from the State for each Worker assigned this way.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1

### Voice of middle class workers ×1
- **Italian:** La voce della classe media
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add from the supply to the bag a number of your Voting cubes equal to 2 plus the number of Public and Capitalist Companies in which your Workers are assigned.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

## Uncertain
- Nothing was unreadable. All Legitimacy symbols on these sheets are yellow (Middle) circles; there are no pentagons (tokens) on any Middle Class card.
- Index errors in the source: it lists "Programma d'istruzione superiore" with the Italian name repeated as its English name. "Higher education program" is taken from the Capitalist index entry for the same Italian name. The index spells "State Scolarship"; corrected here to "State Scholarship".
- "Bill marker" translates "segnalino proposta di legge", and "in-effect section" (Protect the status quo) translates "sezione in vigore". These are literal renderings, not checked against the official English card text.
- "Tariff" (Import subsidy) translates "dazio", which is not in the glossary.
- Fake news and Interest groups do not say where the other classes' cubes go beyond what is translated above; nothing was added.
