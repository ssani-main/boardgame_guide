# Capitalist Class action cards (English translation of the unofficial Italian file v2.1 by F. Santoro)

Total: 40 base cards (28 distinct) + 5 Crisis & Control cards (5 distinct) = 45 cards. Source: sheets 1/5 to 5/5 (PDF pages 20-24).

Note: no Capitalist card shows a pentagon (Legitimacy token) symbol; all Legitimacy symbols are blue circles (+N on the State's Legitimacy track for the Capitalist class).

### Bid rigging ×1
- **Italian:** Manipolazione dell'offerta
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell up to 6 Luxury to the State for ₩10 each. Then gain 1 Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1 for every 3 Luxury sold

### Business expansion ×2
- **Italian:** Attività in espansione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Build 2 Companies. Then add 2 new Companies to the Market.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Business grants ×1
- **Italian:** Sovvenzioni alle imprese
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Gain ₩5 from the State for each Company you own.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1 for every 3 of your Companies

### Buy private island ×1
- **Italian:** Compra un'isola privata
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay ₩50 to the State from your Capital. Gain 7 VP.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Change of Government agenda ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Cambio di agenda governativa
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If there are 3 or fewer Bills on the board, discard one Bill of another player. Then you Propose another Bill. Finally, that player Proposes another Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Competitive wages ×2
- **Italian:** Stipendi competitivi
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Set the Wages of one of your non-operational Companies to the maximum (L3). Then choose a Public Sector Company with **the same number of Workers** that the Company you chose first needs, and Assign those Workers to the latter (your chosen Company).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Covid-19 vaccine R&D funding ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Finanziamenti alla ricerca per il vaccino anti Covid-19
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain ₩15 from the State for each operational Health Company you own.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1 for each of your Companies described by this card

### Endorse political campaign ×1
- **Italian:** Appoggia una campagna politica
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay ₩15 and add 6 of your Voting cubes from the supply to the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Exit strategy ×2
- **Italian:** Strategia d'uscita
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell one of your Companies **without automation** (that is, without the printed gear symbol on it or an added gear token) for double its cost.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Extra shift ×2
- **Italian:** Turno di lavoro extra
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose one of your operational, non-automated Companies (those that require Workers, even if they have a gear token on them), pay the Wages and carry out one production of that Company.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Fake news ×1
- **Italian:** Fake news
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Draw 6 Voting cubes from the bag. Return up to 4 of them to the supply and the rest to the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign market insight ×2
- **Italian:** Conoscenza del mercato estero
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Reveal the next 2 Export cards. You may replace the current Export card with one of the two just revealed. Then discard the unused Export cards and Sell to the Foreign Market.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign partner ×2
- **Italian:** Partner estero
- **Requirement:** 6B/6C
- **Effect:** Make a Business Deal. Then you may Sell Food and/or Luxury to the Foreign Market.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign recruitment ×2
- **Italian:** Reclutamento di personale straniero
- **Requirement:** 7B/7C
- **Effect:** Choose one of your non-operational Companies. Spend ₩10 and Assign to it the matching Workers **from the supply**.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Global branding ×1
- **Italian:** Branding globale
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell to the Foreign Market. You may also sell up to 6 Luxury for ₩10 each.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Global food crisis ×1
- **Italian:** Crisi alimentare globale
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Sell to the Foreign Market. You may also sell up to 4 Food for ₩15 each.

### Higher education program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma d'istruzione superiore

### Industrialization ×1
- **Italian:** Industrializzazione
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Build a Company in the Food or Luxury sector, paying half its cost rounded up. The State pays the remaining part.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1

### Interest groups ×1
- **Italian:** Gruppi d'interesse
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Reveal Voting cubes from the bag until you have drawn 3 of other classes. Replace them with the same number of your own Voting cubes taken from the supply, and put these (your cubes) into the bag together with the remaining revealed cubes.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Investment opportunities ×2
- **Italian:** Opportunità d'investimento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Build a Company paying ₩10 less. **OR** search the Company deck for a Company of your choice and Build it paying ₩10 more. Afterwards, shuffle the Company deck.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Momentum for change ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Verso il cambiamento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 1 Influence for each of your Bill markers on the board. Then Propose a Bill. You **cannot** call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Offshore companies ×2
- **Italian:** Società offshore
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Move half the money (rounded down) from the Revenue area to the Capital area.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Protect the status quo ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Proteggi lo status quo
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose a policy. For the rest of this round, **no** Bill can be proposed for it (place one of your Bill markers in the matching in-force section as a reminder). Then gain 1 Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public opinion polling ×1
- **Italian:** Sondaggio d'opinione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill and reveal 5 Voting cubes from the bag. Then you may call an Immediate Vote on it without spending Influence. If you do, use those 5 revealed Voting cubes instead of drawing new ones. If you don't, put those cubes back in the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Push political agenda ×1
- **Italian:** Spingi per un programma politico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay ₩25 to Propose two Bills. Resolve each action **separately**. You **cannot** propose the same Bill twice in a row.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Radical reforms ×1
- **Italian:** Riforme radicali
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill. You may place your marker **in any space**, even one not adjacent to the current policy. You **cannot** call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Reveal political scandal ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Rivela uno scandalo politico
- **Requirement:** none

- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Technological progress ×3
- **Italian:** Progresso tecnologico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay ₩20 and gain 1 gear (automation) token. **OR** Pay ₩45 and gain 2 gear (automation) tokens.

### Trade protectionism lobby ×1
- **Italian:** Lobby del protezionismo commerciale
- **Requirement:** 6A/6B
- **Effect:** Add 2 Voting cubes from the supply to the bag for each Company you own in the Food sector. If policy **6A** is in force, also count your Companies in the Luxury sector.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Unemployment initiative program ×2
- **Italian:** Programma di riduzione della disoccupazione
- **Requirement:** 4A/4B
- **Effect:** Assign unemployed Workers to one of your non-operational Companies. Gain ₩5 from the State for each Worker assigned this way.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1

## Uncertain
- **Gear token name** (Technological progress, Exit strategy, Extra shift): the cards show only a gear icon ("segnalino ⚙"), no word. I wrote "gear (automation) token"; it is the token that makes a Company automated (glossary: Machinery / automated). Check the official English term on your own cards.
- **Interest groups:** the card does not say where the 3 drawn cubes of other classes go after being replaced (presumably back to their owners' supply). Translated as written.
- **Italian names differ slightly between card and index** for two C&C cards: card "Cambio di agenda governativa" vs index "Cambio di agenda di governo"; card "Finanziamenti alla ricerca per il vaccino anti Covid-19" vs index "Finanziamento della ricerca ...". The card titles are used above.
- **Index quirk:** the Italian index lists "Interest groups" in the Italian column and "Gruppi d'interesse" in the English column (swapped). The card title is "Gruppi d'interesse", so the official English name is taken as "Interest groups".
- **Legitimacy "(4p)":** the source cards show a single set of Legitimacy symbols with no player-count marking; they are listed as printed.
