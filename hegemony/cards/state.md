# State action cards (English translation of the unofficial Italian file v2.1 by F. Santoro)

Total: 45 cards = 40 base + 5 Crisis & Control (C&C).

### A matter of high priority ×2
- **Italian:** Questione di priorità
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Look at the top 2 Event cards of the Event deck and, if possible, place one of them on the board (you must have an empty space on the board, e.g. because you have already resolved an Event). Then, resolve an Event (it may be a different one from the one you just placed).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Agenda-setting ×2
- **Italian:** Imposta il programma politico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 1 Personal Influence for each policy currently in force that matches your current Political Agenda card.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Allowances and subsidies ×2
- **Italian:** Indennità e sussidi
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Provide 20₩ to one class. Gain 1 Legitimacy token of that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** gain a Working, Middle or Capitalist token (the class you provided to)

### Business grants ×1
- **Italian:** Sovvenzioni alle imprese
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Provide 5₩ to the Capitalist class for each of its Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Capitalist +1 for every 3 of its Companies

### Change of Government agenda ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Cambio di agenda governativa
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If there are 3 or fewer Bills on the board, discard one Bill proposed by another player. Then you Propose a Bill. Finally, that player Proposes a Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Construction boom ×1
- **Italian:** Boom del settore edilizio
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** The State, the Working class and the Capitalist class each gain 15₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1, Capitalist +1

### Covid-19 vaccination program ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Programma di vaccinazione anti Covid-19
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Buy Health from the Foreign Market at 5₩ each, up to your current storage capacity. Then, Provide Health to the Working or Middle class, equal to its population. For every 3 Health provided this way, gain +1 Legitimacy with that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working or Middle +1 per 3 Health provided (variable, shown as "*")

### Denial of free speech ×1
- **Italian:** Divieto di libera manifestazione del pensiero
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain up to 2 Media Influence. Then, choose up to 2 opponents. Each of them gives you 1 Influence, and you move back 1 space on their Legitimacy track.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** -1 with each chosen opponent class (up to 2 of Working / Middle / Capitalist; variable, shown as "*")

### Emergency state ×1
- **Italian:** Stato d'emergenza
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If there are at least 3 Bills on the board, choose a policy without a Bill and move its marker (the black one showing which section of the policy is in force) to an adjacent section.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Employment support scheme ×1
- **Italian:** Progetto di sostegno all'occupazione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Provide 15₩ to the Working class and to the Middle class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1, Middle +1

### Foreign financial assistance ×2
- **Italian:** Assistenza finanziaria estera
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 10₩ for each policy currently in force that matches your current Political Agenda card.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign investment program ×2
- **Italian:** Programma d'investimento estero
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Spend 2 Personal Influence to gain 40₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Foreign students ×1
- **Italian:** Studenti stranieri
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Sell up to 8 Education to the Foreign Market at the current local price (i.e. the one set by policy 5. Welfare State - Education).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Geopolitical support ×2
- **Italian:** Supporto geopolitico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain money according to the Foreign Trade policy in force (6. Foreign trade): 6A: 15₩; 6B: 30₩; 6C: 45₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Growth in tourism ×1
- **Italian:** Crescita del turismo
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** The State, the Middle class and the Capitalist class each gain 15₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Middle +1, Capitalist +1

### Healthcare program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma di assistenza sanitaria
- **Requirement:** 4A/4B
- **Effect:** Provide Health to the Working or Middle class, equal to its population. Gain 1 Legitimacy token of that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** gain a Working or Middle token (the class you provided to)

### Higher VAT ×2
- **Italian:** Aumento dell'IVA
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Each player pays 15₩ to the State. Then, the State moves down 1 space on the Legitimacy track of two classes of its choice.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** -1 with two classes of the State's choice (Working / Middle / Capitalist; shown as "*")

### Immediate response ×2
- **Italian:** Risposta immediata
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose an Event and resolve it twice in a row. Each time you resolve it you may make different choices. If the Event asks you to Provide resources and you choose to resolve it twice in favour of the same class, as an exception the resources from both resolutions add up in the State Benefits space.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none (only what the resolved Event gives)

### Literacy program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma di tutoraggio studentesco
- **Requirement:** 5A/5B
- **Effect:** Provide Education to the Working or Middle class, equal to its population. Gain 1 Legitimacy token of that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** gain a Working or Middle token (the class you provided to)

### Medical tourism ×1
- **Italian:** Turismo sanitario
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Sell up to 8 Health to the Foreign Market at the current local price (i.e. the one set by policy 4. Welfare State - Healthcare).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Momentum for change ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Verso il cambiamento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 1 Personal Influence for each of your Bill markers on the board. Then, Propose a Bill. You may **not** call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Nationalization ×1
- **Italian:** Nazionalizzazione
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Remove a non-operational Public Company from the game and replace it with a Capitalist Company of your choice, provided that it is (a) non-operational, (b) on the board and (c) without the gear-shaped symbol (token or printed), and put it in the Public Sector area. Then, you may assign Workers to it. If 1B is in force, pay the Company's cost to the Capitalist class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Pressing matters ×2
- **Italian:** Questioni urgenti
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill. You may call an Immediate Vote without spending Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Privatization ×1
- **Italian:** Privatizzazione
- **Requirement:** 1B/1C
- **Effect:** Sell one of your operational Companies to the Capitalist class at its cost, putting it in the Capitalist Class area. If the Capitalist sells it back, it returns to your Public Sector area.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** gain a Capitalist token

### Protect the status quo ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Proteggi lo status quo
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose a policy. For the rest of this round, **no** Bill may be proposed for it (place one of your Bill markers on its section currently in force as a reminder). Then, gain 1 Personal Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public sector overtime ×2
- **Italian:** Straordinari nel settore pubblico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose one of your Companies. Pay its Workers' Wages and carry out that Company's production.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Quantitative easing ×1
- **Italian:** Quantitative easing
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 25₩. If you have at least one Loan, gain 35₩ instead.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Radical reforms ×1
- **Italian:** Riforme radicali
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill. You may place your marker on **any space**, even one not adjacent to the current policy. You may **not** call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Reveal political scandal ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Rivela uno scandalo politico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Take 2 Influence from another player.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Shift focus ×2
- **Italian:** Spostare l'attenzione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Look at the top 2 Event cards of the deck. You may discard, without penalty, one of the Events on the board and replace it with one of those cards. Then, resolve an Event (it may be a different one from the one you just replaced, if you replaced one).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Step for representation ×2
- **Italian:** Un passo verso la rappresentanza politica
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Provide 1 Influence and 2 Voting cubes to one class. Gain +2 Legitimacy with that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +2, Middle +2 or Capitalist +2 (the class you provided to)

### Supplemental income program ×1
- **Italian:** Reddito supplementare
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Provide 1₩ to the Working or Middle class for each of its employed Workers. For every 10₩ you provide, gain +1 Legitimacy with that class.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working or Middle +1 per 10₩ provided (variable, shown as "*")

### Unemployment initiative program ×1
- **Italian:** Programma di riduzione della disoccupazione
- **Requirement:** 4A/4B
- **Effect:** Assign unemployed Workers to one of your non-operational Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

## Uncertain
- **Nationalization:** condition (c) uses a small gear-shaped icon, not a word ("senza ⚙ (segnalino o stampato)"). It most likely means the Automation/machinery symbol, but the card doesn't name it. Translated as "without the gear-shaped symbol (token or printed)".
- **Denial of free speech** and **Reveal political scandal:** the Italian just says "influenza", without saying Personal or Media. Translated as plain "Influence".
- **Circles marked "*"** (Covid-19 vaccination program, Denial of free speech, Higher VAT, Supplemental income program) have no fixed number. The Legitimacy lines for these cards come from the card text.
- **Spelling of names:** taken as written in the index (e.g. "Change of Government agenda", "Covid-19 vaccination program", "A matter of high priority"). The card itself names the C&C card "Cambio di agenda governativa", but the index on page 7 has "Cambio di agenda di governo". Both map to the same English name.
