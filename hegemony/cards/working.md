# Working Class action cards (English translation of the unofficial Italian file v2.1 by F. Santoro)

Total: 45 cards = 40 base + 5 Crisis & Control (C&C). 30 distinct cards. Source: sheets "Classe operaia foglio 1/5 to 5/5" (PDF pages 10-14); English names from the index on page 4.

Notes: Underlined actions on the cards (Propose a Bill, Assign, Buy Goods & Services, Strike, etc.) follow the normal rules for that action, except where the card changes them. Every Legitimacy symbol on these cards is a red circle (Working class, +1 on the State's Legitimacy track). No Working card shows a pentagon (Legitimacy token).

### Affordable housing ×1
- **Italian:** Abitazioni a prezzo accessibile
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Pay 20₩ to the Capitalist class and gain 5 VP, OR pay 10₩ to the Middle class and gain 3 VP.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Boost domestic tourism ×2
- **Italian:** Incentivo al turismo interno
- **Requirement:** 1A/1B
- **Effect:** Buy Luxury from another player, up to your Population value, paying half the cost, rounded up. The State pays the rest.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1

### Change of Government agenda ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Cambio di agenda governativa
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If there are 3 or fewer Bills on the board, discard one Bill belonging to another player. Then you Propose another Bill. Finally, that player Proposes another Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Cooperative farm ×2
- **Italian:** Cooperativa agricola
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If you have at least 3 unemployed Workers, start a Cooperative Farm, put it next to your board and Assign 3 of those Workers to it.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Fake news ×1
- **Italian:** Fake news
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Draw 6 Voting cubes from the bag. Put up to 4 of them into the reserve and the rest back into the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### General strike ×2
- **Italian:** Sciopero generale
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Strike. You may place one additional Strike token for each active Trade Union you have.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Healthcare benefits ×2
- **Italian:** Assistenza sanitaria
- **Requirement:** 4B/4C
- **Effect:** Buy Health from the State, up to your Population value, paying half the cost, rounded up.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1

### Healthcare movement ×1
- **Italian:** Movimento per l'assistenza sanitaria
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add 2 Voting cubes from the reserve to the bag. Then Propose a Bill on Welfare - Healthcare (4. Welfare State - Healthcare).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Highlight social issues ×1
- **Italian:** Evidenzia i problemi sociali
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Buy 3 Influence from the State for 15₩.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1

### Immigration ×2
- **Italian:** Immigrazione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** If you have at least 4 unemployed Workers, remove up to 2 of them and lower your Population to match (not below the minimum). Gain 5₩ for each unskilled Worker and 10₩ for each skilled Worker removed this way.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Immigration reform ×1
- **Italian:** Riforma dell'immigrazione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add 2 of your Voting cubes from the reserve to the bag. Then Propose a Bill on Immigration (7. Immigration).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Interest groups ×1
- **Italian:** Gruppi d'interesse
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Reveal Voting cubes from the bag until you have drawn 3 cubes of other classes. Replace those 3 with the same number of your own Voting cubes taken from the reserve, and put your cubes into the bag together with the other revealed cubes.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Labor market deregulation ×2
- **Italian:** Deregolamentazione del mercato del lavoro
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Assign any number of unemployed Workers. If 2C is in effect, you may also reassign any number of already assigned Workers to other Companies.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Momentum for change ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Verso il cambiamento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Gain 1 Influence for each of your Bill markers on the board. Then Propose a Bill. You cannot call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Need for change ×3
- **Italian:** Bisogno di cambiamento
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill. Then Propose a second Bill, different from the first one.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Proletarians of the world, unite! ×2
- **Italian:** Proletari del mondo, unitevi!
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** From the reserve, add to the bag a number of Voting cubes equal to your Population.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Protect the status quo ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Proteggi lo status quo
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose a policy. For the rest of this round, no Bill can be proposed for it (put one of your Bill markers in that policy's in-effect section as a reminder). Then gain 1 Influence.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public opinion polling ×1
- **Italian:** Sondaggio d'opinione
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill and reveal 5 Voting cubes from the bag. Then you may call an Immediate Vote on that Bill without spending Influence. If you do, use those 5 revealed Voting cubes instead of drawing new ones. If you don't, put those cubes back into the bag.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Public sector overtime ×2
- **Italian:** Straordinari nel settore pubblico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose an operational Public Sector Company. The State pays the Wages of the Workers assigned there and runs production with that Company. Then you may Buy Goods & Services from the State of the type that Company produces.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Radical reforms ×1
- **Italian:** Riforme radicali
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Propose a Bill. You may place your marker on any space, even one that is not next to the current policy. You cannot call an Immediate Vote on this Bill.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Reveal political scandal ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Rivela uno scandalo politico
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Take 2 Influence from another player.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Rising Covid-19 Cases ×1 · C&C
- **Italian:** Aumento dei casi di covid-19
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Get Health from the State without paying for it, up to half your Population value, rounded up. Then you may Buy Health from any number of sources, up to your full Population value from each of them.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1

### Signing bonus ×2
- **Italian:** Bonus d'assunzione
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Assign up to 4 unemployed Workers. For each Worker assigned to a Company this way, gain 4₩ from that Company's owner.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Specialization ×2
- **Italian:** Specializzazione
- **Requirement:** 7B/7C
- **Effect:** Take a skilled Worker of your choice from the reserve and place it in the unemployed area. Then Assign up to 3 Workers.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### State Scholarship ×2
- **Italian:** Borsa di studio statale
- **Requirement:** 5B/5C
- **Effect:** Buy Education from the State, up to your Population value, paying half the cost, rounded up.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1

### Student movement ×1
- **Italian:** Movimento studentesco
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add 2 Voting cubes from the reserve to the bag. Then Propose a Bill on Welfare - Education (5. Welfare State - Education).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Supplemental income program ×2
- **Italian:** Reddito supplementare
- **Requirement:** 2B/2C
- **Effect:** Gain 1₩ from the State for each of your assigned Workers. Then you may Buy Goods & Services.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1 for every 10 of your assigned Workers

### Unemployment benefits ×2
- **Italian:** Indennità di disoccupazione
- **Requirement:** 4A/4B
- **Effect:** Gain 5₩ from the State for each of your unemployed Workers. Then you may Buy Goods & Services from the State.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** Working +1 for every 3 of your unemployed Workers

### Workers' movement ×1
- **Italian:** Movimento per i lavoratori
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Add 2 of your Voting cubes from the reserve to the bag. Then Propose a Bill on the Labor Market (2. Labor Market).
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

### Workplace accident ×1
- **Italian:** Incidente sul lavoro
- **Requirement:** none
- **Effect:** Choose an industry sector (Food, Luxury, Health, Education or Influence). Take 8₩ from another player for each of their Companies in that sector where your Workers are assigned.
- **Legitimacy (4p):** none

## Uncertain
- The index on page 4 misspells two names ("Healtchcare benefits", "State Scolarship"). I corrected them to "Healthcare benefits" and "State Scholarship". Otherwise the names are written exactly as the index gives them.
- In the yellow C&C rows of the index, the last row has its columns swapped ("Momentum for change" sits in the Italian column and "Verso il cambiamento" in the English one). The card itself is titled "Verso il cambiamento", so it is listed here as Momentum for change.
- Healthcare movement and Student movement say "2 cubi voto" (2 Voting cubes), not "2 tuoi cubi voto" (2 of your Voting cubes) as Workers' movement and Immigration reform do. The translation follows the wording exactly as printed. The cubes are presumably your own.
- Interest groups does not say where the 3 replaced cubes of other classes go. Presumably they go back to the reserve. The card text only says to put your own cubes and the other revealed cubes into the bag.
- Affordable housing uses "PV" (punti vittoria), which is translated as VP (victory points).
- Everything else read cleanly. No card text was unreadable.
