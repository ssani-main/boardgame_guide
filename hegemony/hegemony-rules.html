Hegemony is an asymmetric card-driven game for 2–4 players. Workers, the middle class, capitalists and the State fight over wages, taxes and welfare for 5 rounds. Each class wins in its own way, and every policy change is decided at the ballot box.

First game? The rulebook suggests 2 players (Capitalist vs Working) before adding the Middle Class and the State. Suggested seating: Capitalist beside the State, Middle Class beside the Working Class.

    


    
4 classes enter the ring!


    
    

      

        
The cast

        
2 players: Capitalist vs Working. 3 adds the Middle Class, 4 adds the State. Turn order is always Working → Middle → Capitalist → State. Round-by-round playbooks ↓

      

      


        

          
01

          

            
Working Class

            
goal · raise prosperity

            
Sell your labour, then spend wages on Food, Health, Education and Luxury. Each Prosperity step scores VP equal to its new value, and Prosperity drops 1 every round.

            

              Start: 30₩, 1 Influence, population 10

              Basic: Propose Bill · Assign Workers (up to 3) · Buy Goods & Services · Strike · Demonstration · Political Pressure (+3 cubes)

              Free: Use Healthcare (+2 VP, +1 worker) · Use Education (train a worker) · Use Luxury · Swap Workers · Receive Benefits · Pay Off Loan

              Trade Unions: a skilled worker plus 4 others in that industry gives +1 Influence and +2 VP per round

              Board: top track shows workers → Population (10 workers = 3). Prosperity 0–10, 5 Trade Union slots, Income, Goods & Services
            

          

        


        

          
02

          

            
Middle Class

            
goal · prosperity + small business

            
Works in other companies and runs up to 8 of its own. "Buys" from itself for free. Prosperity drops 2 every round, so it has to keep feeding it.

            

              Start: 40₩, 1 Influence, 1 Food, 1 Health, population 10

              Basic: Propose Bill · Assign Workers · Build / Sell Company · Sell to Foreign Market (+1 VP per deal) · Buy · Extra Shift · Political Pressure

              Free: Use Health / Education / Luxury · Adjust Prices · Adjust Wages · Swap · Receive Benefits · Pay Off Loan

              Scoring: +1 Prosperity if Prosperity is lower than your fully staffed companies

              Board: Population track, a Prosperity track that tops out at 7, 4 storages (8 each) with price slots (Food 9/12/15, others 5/8/10), Income, Goods & Services
            

          

        


        

          
03

          

            
Capitalist Class

            
goal · maximise capital

            
Builds up to 12 companies, sets wages and sells at home or abroad. Income lands in Revenue and moves to Capital after taxes. The Wealth table scores Capital every round, plus 3 VP for each space of a new high.

            

              Start: 120₩ in Revenue, 4 companies, Free Trade Zone

              Basic: Propose Bill · Build / Sell Company · Sell to Foreign Market · Business Deal · Lobby (30₩ → 3 Influence) · Political Pressure

              Free: Adjust Prices · Adjust Wages · Give Bonus (5₩, commits workers) · Buy Storage (20₩) · Receive Benefits · Pay Off Loan

              Taxes: Employment (operational companies × Tax Multiplier), then Corporate

              Board: Revenue and Capital boxes, the Wealth table (10₩ … 500₩+), storages (Food 8, others 12) with price slots, plus unlimited Influence
            

          

        


        

          
04

          

            
The State

            
goal · keep every class on side

            
Runs the public companies and has 3 Legitimacy tracks, one per class. Each round it scores the sum of its two lowest tracks, then halves all three, so balance beats one big peak.

            

              Start: 120₩ Treasury, Legitimacy 2/2/2, 1 Influence, 2 Events, 1 Political Agenda

              Basic: Propose Bill · Event Action · Sell to Foreign Market · Meet with MPs (2 Influence → +1 Legitimacy) · Extra Tax (10₩ from each class) · Campaign (up to 3 Media → Personal Influence)

              Free: Adjust Wages · Pay Off Loan

              No cubes: votes only with Personal Influence

              Board: 3 Legitimacy tracks (red Working, yellow Middle, blue Capitalist), with the Influence you gain printed above each column; welfare reminders; Food / Luxury / Influence storage
            

          

        


      

    


    
One round, five phases


    
    

      
The round

      

        

          1
          
Preparation

          
Skipped in round 1. Pay 5₩ interest per Loan, Prosperity drops (Working −1, Middle −2), draw back up to 7, refill Markets and Events, new workers arrive (+ Immigration).

        

        

          2
          
Action

          
Each player takes 5 turns: one Main Action (play a card, or discard it for a Basic Action) and one Free Action. Keep 2 cards for next round.

        

        

          3
          
Production

          
Reverse order. Pay wages (once per company), produce, then Food = Population. Check the IMF, then pay taxes to the Treasury.

        

        

          4
          
Elections

          
Refill the bag, then vote on every pending Bill from Policy 1 to 7. Skipped if no Bill is pending.

        

        

          5
          
Scoring

          
Trade Unions, the Middle Class's free Prosperity, the Capitalist's Wealth, and the State's Legitimacy and Agenda. The State pays penalties for unresolved Events.

        

      

      
Loans are never voluntary. You only take one (+50₩) when forced to pay what you can't afford, and paying one off costs 50₩. Workers lying down are committed: they can't move, their wage can't drop and their company can't be sold until it produces.

    


    
    

      
Elections

      

        

          01
          
Propose a Bill

          
Put a marker on the section next to the current one, on a policy with no marker yet. Pay 1 Influence for an Immediate Vote. You have 3 markers, so at most 3 pending Bills.

        

        

          02
          
Fill the bag

          
Working adds ½ Population, Capitalist ½ operational companies, Middle the higher of the two (all rounded up). In 2p, 5 Middle Class cubes still go in. The State adds no cubes; it gains Influence from its lowest Legitimacy track.

        

        

          03
          
Vote

          
Declare For or Against, draw 5 cubes, then everyone spends hidden Influence (+1 vote each). Ties pass.

          passed → proposer +3 VP · each contributing supporter +1 VP
        

      

      
The winning side's cubes and all spent Influence are discarded; the losing side's cubes go back into the bag. In 2p, Middle Class cubes count toward the 5 but vote for nobody.

      
    


    
Class playbooks


    
    

      

        
How each class plays

        
Turns first, then what each class does in every phase of a round, with worked examples. All numbers come from the rulebook's own examples.

      


      
      

        

          
1
Pick a card
You hold 7. Every card can be used in one of two ways, so even a useless card still buys an action.

          
2
Main action
Play it for its effect, but only if its requirement is met (usually a policy section). Or discard it to take one Basic Action from your player aid.

          
3
Free action
One free action, before or after the main action. This is where Prosperity, prices, wages and loans happen.

          
4
Pass
Next class in order: W → M → C → S. After 5 turns each, keep the last 2 cards. Next round you draw 5 back up to 7.

        

        

          

            
Example · a full Working Class turn

            
Jim (Population 5) discards a card to Buy Goods & Services: 5 Health from the State and 3 from the Capitalist. As his free action he takes Use Healthcare:

            
spend 5 Health (= Population)
Prosperity → space "5"   +5 VP
Healthcare bonus          +2 VP
+1 gray worker → Population track +1

          

          

            
Example · playing a card for its effect

            
Jim plays State Scholarship. Its requirement is met, so he buys Education at half price (half of the total, rounded up: at 5B, 5 × 5₩ = 25₩ → 13₩), paid to the State.

            
4 players: card shows a Working Class Legitimacy symbol
→ the State moves its Working Class track +1
(ignored if the card is only discarded for a Basic Action)

          

        

      


      
      

        
W
Working Class
You never own companies. Your workers earn wages, and you turn wages into Prosperity.

        

          

            Your round
            

              
Prep
Pay 5₩ per Loan · Prosperity −1 (no VP lost) · draw 5 · +2 gray workers, +1 or +2 more from Immigration at 7B / 7C.

              
Action
5 turns. Usual rhythm: Assign Workers to open jobs, Buy Health / Education / Luxury, then spend them with a free Use action. Strike when owners pay only L1/L2; Demonstrate when there aren't enough jobs.

              
Production
Strikes resolve first (struck companies don't produce, +1 Influence each). Collect wages, +1 Influence per Trade Union. Then you must buy Food = Population (Loan if short) and pay income tax.

              
Elections
Add cubes = ½ Population (rounded up). More workers means more cubes in the bag.

              
Scoring
+2 VP per Trade Union. Most of your VP comes from the Use actions you take during the round.

            

          

          

            Worked examples
            

              
Buying from two sources

              
Population 5, so you can take up to 5 from each of 2 sources.

              
State (4B)      5 Health × 5₩ = 25₩
Capitalist      3 Health × 8₩ = 24₩   (only 3 in stock)
total           8 Health for 49₩

              
Foreign Luxury at 6B (Population 6): 6 × 6₩ = 36₩ to the supply + 6 × 3₩ = 18₩ tariff to the State.

            

            

              
Prosperity is where the points are

              
Use Education: 4 bought + 1 left over = 5 = Population
Prosperity 5 → 6        +6 VP
swap a gray worker for a skilled (purple) one

              
Each step pays the new track value, so later steps are worth more. Prosperity drops 1 each round, so keep climbing.

            

            

              
Trade Union

              
2 workers on a Capitalist farm, then Assign Workers puts 2 gray workers on Middle Class farms. That's 4 in Agriculture, so the green worker joins the Agriculture union: +1 Influence and +2 VP every round. If a worker leaves and you drop below 4, the union dissolves.

            

            

              
Demonstration

              
unemployed 5  vs  open slots 2   (need ≥ 2 more) ✓
penalty if not fixed = 5 unemployed + 2 unions = 7 VP
you split it among the others, but each player loses at most
their empty company spaces (Capitalist with 8 of 12 → max 4)

              
The Capitalist builds a 3-slot company and hires 3 of them, so the condition is no longer met and the token is discarded.

            

            

              
Income tax

              
Labor Market 2C × Taxation 3B → 2₩ per Population
Population 5 → 10₩ to the Treasury

            

            
Watch: Food = Population every round is mandatory. Healthcare adds a worker, which means more food to buy but also more voting cubes. A Socialist board (A) pays 1/4/8/12/18 VP at the end.

          

        

      


      
      

        
M
Middle Class
Half worker, half owner. Your own shops feed your people, and the surplus gets exported.

        

          

            Your round
            

              
Prep
Pay 5₩ per Loan · Prosperity −2 · draw 5 · refill your Market to 3 (you may discard first) · +1 gray and +1 skilled worker of your choice, plus Immigration.

              
Action
5 turns. Build small companies with your own workers (max 8), Assign workers into others' companies, Extra Shift for instant output, Buy (yourself counts as a source) and Export (+1 VP per deal).

              
Production
First you're paid for workers in others' companies. Then your own companies produce: no wage for your own workers, but you pay any Working Class employee. Storage holds 8 each. Cover Food = Population from your own stock first, then pay income + employment tax.

              
Elections
Cubes = the higher of ½ Population or ½ operational companies, rounded up.

              
Scoring
If Prosperity is lower than your fully operational companies (2 workers each), +1 Prosperity for free, with its VP.

            

          

          

            Worked examples
            

              
Buying from yourself

              
Population 5, with 1 Health already in Goods & Services.

              
State (4B)     5 Health × 5₩ = 25₩
own storage    3 Health → moved for free (counts as source 2)
total          9 Health, so the Capitalist can't be a 3rd source

              
Your own products must be "bought" this way before you can use them.

            

            

              
Exporting: money and VP

              
storage: 4 Food · 8 Luxury · 6 Health
3 Food   → 35₩      2 Luxury → 10₩
6 Luxury → 35₩      5 Health → 35₩
115₩ + 4 VP   (each offer once per action; 1 Food stays)

            

            

              
Two taxes

              
Income: workers in 2 Capitalist + 1 public company = 3
        2A × 3C → 5₩ × 3 = 15₩
Employment: 6 own operational × Tax Multiplier 5 = 30₩

            

            

              
Free Prosperity at scoring

              
Round 1: Prosperity 2, with 3 companies but the Doctor's Office has no employee, so only 2 count. 2 is not lower than 2, so no bonus. Later: 4 fully operational vs Prosperity 3, so +1 Prosperity (to the space showing 4) = +4 VP.

            

            
Watch: Prosperity falls 2 every round and the track tops out at 7. Only goods still in storage can be exported. At the end you score 1/3/6/10/15 VP for B policies, plus leftover stock and 1 VP per 15₩.

          

        

      


      
      

        
C
Capitalist Class
Build, hire, produce, sell. VP comes from Capital at the end of each round and from beating your record.

        

          

            Your round
            

              
Prep
Pay 5₩ per Loan (from Capital) · draw 5 · refill your Market to 4 · new Business Deals (0 / 1 / 2 by Policy 6) and a new Export card, before the other classes get workers.

              
Action
5 turns. Build (max 12) and staff right away from Unemployed, Sell to Foreign Market, Business Deal, Lobby (30₩ → 3 Influence). Free actions set prices and wages, Give Bonus, Buy Storage.

              
Production
Pay each company's wage once to the worker owner. Produce into storage (Food 8, others 12). Extra Food/Luxury can go to the Free Trade Zone. Others may buy your Food. Then Employment tax, then Corporate tax.

              
Elections
Cubes = ½ operational companies, rounded up (automated ones count).

              
Scoring
Revenue → Capital. Score the Wealth table for your Capital, plus 3 VP per space the Wealth marker moves to a new high.

            

          

          

            Worked examples
            

              
Build and hire in one action

              
The Institute of Technology costs 20₩ and has orange, gray and gray slots. Mike hires the Working Class orange and gray workers. The third slot wants unskilled workers first, but no other Working Class gray worker is free, and he can't mix in a Middle Class one (all workers must be from one class), so he takes the Working Class purple worker.

            

            

              
Exporting

              
storage: 4 Food · 12 Luxury · 11 Education
3 Food → 25₩    4 Lux → 25₩    8 Lux → 45₩
3 Edu  → 20₩    5 Edu → 35₩
150₩ from 5 offers (1 Food + 3 Edu left: each offer once)

            

            

              
Taxes: order matters

              
Employment: 7 operational (of 8) × multiplier 6 = 42₩
Corporate:  Revenue left 81₩, Taxation 3A → bracket 50–99 = 24₩

            

            

              
Wealth table across 3 rounds

              
R1  Capital  57₩ → 3 VP + 3 new spaces × 3 = 12 VP
R2  Capital  34₩ → 2 VP  (marker never moves back)
R3  Capital 166₩ → 7 VP + 4 new spaces × 3 = 19 VP

            

            

              
Forced loan

              
Wages of 75₩ against 25₩ Revenue + 40₩ Capital. You can't cover it, so you take a Loan: +50₩ to Capital. You pay 5₩ interest next round, and paying it off costs 50₩. Any Loan still held at the end costs −5 VP.

            

            
Watch: Strikes hit companies paying L1/L2, and Demonstrations punish empty company spaces. Neoliberal (C) policies pay 1/4/8/12/18 VP at the end; leftover stock scores too.

          

        

      


      
      

        
S
The State
4 players only. You win by keeping your weakest relationship strong, not your best one.

        

          

            Your round
            

              
Prep
Pay 5₩ per Loan · reveal 2 Events · reveal a new Political Agenda · draw 5.

              
Action
5 turns. Solve Events (Provide to a class), Meet with MPs (2 Influence → +1 Legitimacy), Campaign (up to 3 Media → Personal Influence), Extra Tax when broke. Meanwhile the classes buy your services and the money goes to the Treasury.

              
Production
Pay public wages from the Treasury and produce services (storage = production + 6). Check IMF, then collect everyone's taxes.

              
Elections
No cubes. Gain Personal Influence printed above your lowest Legitimacy track. If you hold any Influence you must pick a side in every vote.

              
Scoring
Unresolved Event penalties → VP = sum of the two lowest tracks → halve all (round up) → +1 per Legitimacy token → +1 VP per Agenda match.

            

          

          

            Worked examples
            

              
Cheap welfare pays

              
4B: Middle Class buys 6 Health × 5₩ = 30₩ → +1 VP
5A: Working Class (Population 7) takes 7 Education free
    → +1 Legitimacy per 3 tokens = +2 Working

            

            

              
Card symbols move your tracks

              
The Middle Class plays Health Crisis and sells you 5 Health at 10₩ = 50₩ from the Treasury. The symbol gives +1 Middle Class Legitimacy (6 Health would have given +2).

            

            

              
Event "Declining Birth Rate": Provide Health

              
card: Provide Health = ½ the class's Population (rounded up)
Working Class (Population 7) → 4 Health
you have 3 → buy 1 from the Capitalist (allowed when Providing)
place 4 in Working Class State Benefits → +1 Working Legitimacy
Jim takes them later (Receive Benefits) → +1 VP for you

              
Alternative: Middle Class (Population 6) gets 3 Health → +1 Middle Class Legitimacy. Ignore the Event and both tracks drop 1 at scoring. Some other Events give a Legitimacy token instead: +1 now and +1 every round after halving.

            

            

              
Scoring the State

              
unresolved Event → Middle −1, Capitalist −1
tracks W6 · M4 · C5 → 4 + 5 = 9 VP
halve (round up)   → 3 · 2 · 3
+ tokens (W, M)    → 4 · 3 · 3
Agenda: 3 policies match → +3 VP

            

            

              
Extra Tax has a price

              
You take 10₩ from each class, and your two lowest tracks drop by 1. Jim has only 7₩, so he must take a Loan. You can't use it if those tracks are already at 1.

            

            
Watch: don't let the Treasury run dry. At Fiscal C a single Loan you can't repay (55₩) by the Check IMF step brings in the IMF; at A or B it takes 2. You need allies or Influence to pass your own bills. At the end: 1 VP per 30₩ in the Treasury.

Reader mail

    

      
Rules questions

      

        

          Q.01Is the wage paid per worker?
          
No. The wage on a company card is paid once for all its workers, to the player who owns them.

        

        

          Q.02Does a wage rise from a policy change commit workers?
          
No. Workers are committed only when assigned, or when a player raises the wage with a Free Action.

        

        

          Q.03How does "half price" work?
          
It halves the total, rounded up: 5 Health at 5₩ costs 25₩, so you pay 13₩. The State still gets its welfare VP.

        

        

          Q.04What if Prosperity is already maxed?
          
The marker stays put, but you still score the VP of that last space each time you gain Prosperity.

        

        

          Q.05Can a struck company be sold?
          
Yes, if its workers aren't committed. If a struck company is empty when strikes resolve, the token is removed and gives no Influence.

        

        

          Q.06Do card-forced policy changes give VP?
          
No. VP for policy changes only come from elections (the proposer's +3 and supporters' +1).
