## Faction overview texts

Picture: `intro-<faction>.jpg` (the backs of the faction overview cards).

- **Marquise de Cat** scores by spending **wood** to place **buildings**, scoring more for more of one building type.
  Recruiters add warriors and card draw, sawmills give wood, workshops craft. The **Keep** stops others placing pieces
  in its clearing; while it stands, **Field Hospitals** let her spend cards to return dying warriors to the keep.
- **Eyrie Dynasties** score each turn by how many **roosts** are on the map. They must add to and carry out their
  **Decree** each turn, or fall into **turmoil**, losing points, their **leader** and their Decree. **Lords of the Forest:**
  they rule tied clearings.
- **Woodland Alliance** scores by placing **sympathy**, which needs **supporters**. Oppressing sympathetic clearings
  feeds them supporters. Supporters fund **revolts** that wipe a clearing and found a **base**; bases improve card draw
  and train **officers** for free actions. **Guerrilla War:** they defend with the higher roll.
- **Vagabond** scores by giving cards to friendly factions, beating warriors of hostile ones, and completing
  **quests**. **Items** make him stronger; crafting an item he wants wins his favour. Attack him when he is short on
  swords and he spends a turn repairing in the forest. **Lone Wanderer:** not a warrior, never rules. **Nimble:** moves
  regardless of rule.
