# Root base deck (54 cards)

**Source:** card names and effect texts from Leder Games' official card library (cards.ledergames.com, data file
`rootbasegame.yml` in github.com/LederCards/cards). Crafting costs, items and points were read off the official
card images in the same repository. Card pictures: `../img/cards/card-*.jpg`.

**Copy counts are not printed in any official source.** The library lists each distinct card once (42 faces). The
counts below are the standard deck composition. They match the four suit totals on the BoardGameGeek wiki (14 bird,
14 fox, 13 mouse, 13 rabbit) and the Law's own counts (5 ambush with two birds, 4 dominance). Of the 12 duplicates,
the mouse suit has 11 faces and needs exactly two more cards; we list Codebreakers ×2 and Scouting Party ×2 to
reach 13 (marked *).

**Reading the table:** *Cost* is the crafting cost: the suits of crafting pieces you must activate. "Any" is the
three-colour "?" icon. *Type*: **item** = immediate (take the item from the map supply, score the points, discard);
**persistent** = stays in your play area; **one-shot** = immediate effect, then discard; **—** = cannot be crafted.

## Notes from the Law

- Ambush and dominance cards cannot be crafted (2.1.2, 2.1.3).
- You cannot craft a persistent effect you already have in your play area (4.1.4).
- Crafted items go to your Crafted Items box; the Vagabond can take one when he Aids you (9.5.4).
- The Eyrie score only 1 point per crafted item (Disdain for Trade) unless their leader is the Builder.
